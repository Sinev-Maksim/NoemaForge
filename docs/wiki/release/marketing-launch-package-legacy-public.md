# NoemaForge public launch and marketing package

Status: merged in `0.29.14`.
Source: `docs/research_sources/0.29.14/marketing.md`.

## Core message

NoemaForge is positioned as an open, local-first AI operating layer rather than a single chatbot, API wrapper, or cloud-only assistant. The public pitch emphasizes:

- AI as a system, not an API.
- Local ownership of memory, identity, tools, and execution.
- Non-commercial use inside NoemaForge is free and unrestricted.
- Commercial pricing should remain accessible for small teams and scale with organization size/profit.
- Donations are framed as support for inventors, curious minds, non-standard engineers, learners, and society.

## Pricing model to preserve

| Segment | Intended fee | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Non-commercial NoemaForge use | Free | No usage restriction inside NoemaForge boundaries. |
| Up to 4k EUR | 1 EUR / seat / month | Low barrier for tiny teams. |
| Up to 7.5k EUR | 1.5 EUR / seat / month | Early commercial tier. |
| 7.5k–20k EUR | 2 EUR / seat / month | Small business tier. |
| Above 20k EUR | 0.1% of Net Profit / seat / month | Needs legal/financial wording before public release. |
